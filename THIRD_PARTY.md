## NetBird client

The `netbird` binary in the image is the unmodified client from NetBird's
release archive for the pinned version (`NETBIRD_VERSION` in the `Dockerfile`),
checked against the SHA-256 committed there before it is unpacked. The client
is BSD-3-Clause (copyright NetBird GmbH & AUTHORS); the archive's own
`LICENSE` and `LICENSES/` are installed at `/usr/share/licenses/netbird/`.
The archive's license map (`LICENSES/REUSE.toml`) puts only NetBird's
`management/`, `signal/` and `relay/` services under AGPL-3.0, none of which
is in the client. Source: <https://github.com/netbirdio/netbird>.

## Alpine Linux base image

The image is `alpine:3.24.2`, pinned by digest, plus the `ca-certificates`
package. Alpine's packages are under their own licenses; the set installed in
the image is below (versions follow the pinned base image and change when it
is rebuilt). The GPL-2.0 components (`busybox`, `apk-tools`, `alpine-baselayout`,
`pax-utils`) are unmodified Alpine builds; their corresponding source is in
Alpine's package repositories at <https://gitlab.alpinelinux.org/alpine/aports>
and <https://dl-cdn.alpinelinux.org/alpine/v3.24/main/>.

## Private Internet Access certificate authority

`applier/pia-ca.rsa.4096.crt` is PIA's certificate authority, copied unchanged
from <https://github.com/pia-foss/manual-connections>, which is MIT-licensed
(copyright 2020 Private Internet Access, Inc.). The notice is
[`applier/PIA-CA-LICENSE.txt`](applier/PIA-CA-LICENSE.txt) (installed at
`/usr/share/licenses/molebridge/PIA-CA-LICENSE.txt`).
