## NetBird client

The `netbird` binary in the image is the unmodified client from NetBird's
release archive for the pinned version (`NETBIRD_VERSION` in the `Dockerfile`),
checked against the SHA-256 committed there before it is unpacked. The client
is BSD-3-Clause (copyright NetBird GmbH & AUTHORS); the archive's own
`LICENSE` and `LICENSES/` are installed at `/usr/share/licenses/netbird/`.
The archive's license map (`LICENSES/REUSE.toml`) puts only NetBird's
`management/`, `signal/` and `relay/` services under AGPL-3.0, none of which
is in the client. Source: <https://github.com/netbirdio/netbird>.
